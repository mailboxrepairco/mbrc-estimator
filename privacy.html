SMS / Text Messaging Terms

If you provide your phone number and opt in, we may send you text messages related to your service request — for example, appointment confirmations, scheduling, quotes, reminders, and follow-up messages. Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of purchasing any goods or services.

We do not sell, rent, or share your information — including mobile phone numbers and SMS opt-in consent — with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are never shared with any third parties for marketing.

How We Share Information

We share information only with trusted service providers who help us operate our business — for example, messaging providers (such as our SMS platform), payment processors, and scheduling or estimating software — and only to the extent needed to provide our services to you. These providers are bound to protect your information. We may also disclose information when required by law. We never sell your personal information.

Data Retention & Security

We retain your information only as long as needed to provide services and meet legal or business requirements. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect your information, though no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your Choices

You may opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP. You may request to access, update, or delete your personal information by contacting us using the details below.
